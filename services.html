Common questions

Before you get in touch

How is this different from a normal web agency?

Most agencies build the site and stop there, or only do AI and automation without touching the website. We're one team that does both: we build and host the site, then layer on automation once it's live and actually earning its keep.

How long does it take to get a website live?

A working first draft, built around your real content, usually lands in days rather than months. Wording and details then get refined with your feedback before launch.

Is AI or automation actually relevant for a business my size?

Often, yes, but not always straight away. The free 2-minute AI Readiness quiz gives a straight recommendation rather than a sales pitch, so you know where you stand before spending anything.

Am I locked into a long contract?

No. Silver and Gold run month to month, with an annual option only if you'd rather pay upfront. AI and automation projects are scoped and quoted individually, so you only commit to what you've agreed.

What happens after the site launches?

Hosting, domain and SSL are sorted, and small monthly updates are included on both plans. Gold adds full ongoing SEO and active social media management on top of Silver's basics.

Do you only work with Yorkshire businesses?

We're based in Yorkshire and that's where our roots are, but we work with local and independent businesses across the UK.
