FAQ

Common questions

What kinds of projects do you take on?

Marketing sites, SaaS dashboards, internal tools, customer portals, and AI-powered web apps. If it runs in a browser and needs to be fast, accessible and maintainable, we can build it.

How long does a typical project take?

A marketing site or landing page: 2–3 weeks. A web app with auth, a database and AI features: 4–8 weeks. You'll get a firm timeline before any work begins.

Do you work with existing designs or create them?

Both. If you have a designer or brand guidelines, we implement faithfully. If you need design work, we build a component system from your brief before writing a line of production code.

Can you integrate with our existing tools?

Yes. We regularly wire up CRMs, ERPs, payment processors, analytics platforms and AI APIs. If it has a REST or GraphQL interface, we can integrate it.

What does hosting look like?

We default to Docker Compose on a VPS, cheap, portable, no vendor lock-in, with automatic HTTPS. We can also deploy to Vercel, Azure or AWS if you prefer a managed platform.

What happens after launch?

You get 30 days of included post-launch support. After that, operate the site yourself (we document everything), or take a monthly retainer for ongoing development.

Ready to start building?

Tell us what you're building and we'll send a fixed-price proposal within 48 hours.
