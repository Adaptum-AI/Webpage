FAQ

Common questions

Does the EU AI Act apply to UK businesses?

Yes, if your AI system is used by people in the EU, or your product is placed on the EU market, the Act applies regardless of where your business is incorporated.

High-risk vs limited-risk AI?

High-risk includes AI in recruitment, credit scoring, biometric ID and healthcare. Limited-risk (like a chatbot) requires transparency disclosures. We help you determine where your systems sit.

Is this legal advice?

No. We provide technical documentation, tooling and governance frameworks. For formal legal opinions, engage a qualified solicitor, we're happy to work alongside your legal team.

How long does a project take?

An initial readiness assessment typically takes 2–3 weeks. A full governance framework is a 4–8 week engagement, depending on complexity.

Do you help with ongoing monitoring?

Yes. We offer a retainer covering regulatory change monitoring, quarterly policy reviews, staff training updates and an annual re-assessment.
