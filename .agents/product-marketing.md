# Product Marketing Context

**Document version:** v1

## Product Overview
**One-liner:** Websites, hosting and the AI that makes them work.
**What it does:** Adaptum.AI designs and builds custom websites for UK local and independent businesses, hosts and maintains them long-term, and layers on AI/automation services (strategy, process automation, knowledge systems, compliance, infrastructure) once the site is live.
**Product category:** Web design agency + AI automation consultancy, bundled as one team.
**Product type:** Service business (not SaaS) — project + retainer model.
**Business model:** Two flat-rate website plans (Silver £250 setup + £120/mo, Gold £300 setup + £250/mo, annual options available) covering build, hosting, SEO and social. AI & automation work is scoped and quoted per project. Paid ads are a free add-on billed per lead. One-off website builds (£500–£1,000) for businesses that want to self-host.

## Target Audience
**Target companies:** Local and independent UK businesses and SMEs — trades, professional services, small retail/hospitality — typically without an in-house marketing or IT function.
**Decision-makers:** Owner-operators and small-business directors (review attributions include Business Owner, Managing Director, Operations Manager, Founder, IT Manager).
**Primary use case:** Need a website that actually generates and follows up leads, not just a digital brochure, and want it hosted/maintained without hiring anyone.
**Jobs to be done:**
- Get a professional, mobile-first web presence live fast (days, not months)
- Stop losing enquiries to slow or manual follow-up
- Explore whether AI/automation is worth it for a small business, without a big budget or a data team
**Use cases:** New business needing a first real site; business with an outdated site needing a rebuild; business with a working site that wants automation layered on (lead follow-up, knowledge base, compliance groundwork).

## Problems & Pain Points
**Core problem:** Existing site (or no site) doesn't generate or follow up leads, and the business has no time or expertise to fix it.
**Why alternatives fall short:**
- Generic web agencies build the site and disappear — no hosting/care, no automation
- AI/automation consultancies don't touch the website itself, or start with jargon-heavy strategy decks instead of a working site
- DIY builders (Wix/Squarespace) are cheap but leave the business to handle hosting, SEO, and follow-up alone
**What it costs them:** Missed enquiries, a site that looks unprofessional next to competitors, hours spent on admin a system could do.
**Emotional tension:** Fear of picking the wrong agency, being oversold, or paying for a website that just sits there.

## Competitive Landscape
**Direct:** Local web design agencies/freelancers — fall short on ongoing hosting/care and never touch automation.
**Secondary:** AI/automation consultancies — fall short because they don't build or own the website, so recommendations sit disconnected from the customer's actual storefront.
**Indirect:** DIY site builders (Wix, Squarespace, GoDaddy) — fall short on lead follow-up, SEO depth, and require the owner's own time.

## Differentiation
**Key differentiators:**
- One team spans both the website build and the AI/automation work — most competitors do one or the other
- Fast first draft (days) with every word still checked by a person before launch
- Flat, transparent pricing published on the site (most agencies quote after a sales call)
- Direct access to the people doing the work, not a ticketing system
**How we do it differently:** Website first (so there's a real asset generating leads), automation layered on once it's earning its keep, not sold as a bundle up front.
**Why that's better:** Customers get a working site fast without being upsold AI they don't need yet, and a clear next step (the free quiz) when they are ready.
**Why customers choose us:** Honesty ("no invented stats, no filler") and speed, backed by real 5-star Google reviews.

## Switching Dynamics
**Push:** Missed leads, an outdated or DIY site, a previous agency that built-and-vanished.
**Pull:** Fast turnaround, transparent pricing, one team for both web and AI.
**Habit:** Inertia — "the current site is fine, we're busy" — and not knowing where to start with AI.
**Anxiety:** Being locked into a contract, being oversold, or the new site not actually generating more enquiries than the old one.
