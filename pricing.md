# Pricing — Adaptum.AI

All prices in GBP. Websites, hosting and AI automation for UK local and independent businesses.

## Silver

- Setup: £250 one-off
- Recurring: £120/month, or £1,200/year if paid annually
- Contract: month to month, no long lock-in; annual option is optional, not required
- Includes: custom-built mobile-first website, hosting/domain/SSL, basic SEO & Google Business setup, light social media presence, small monthly updates
- Lead generation: not active on this plan, so no per-lead fee
- Best for: a business that needs a properly built, hosted site kept running, without active ongoing marketing

## Gold

- Setup: £300 one-off
- Recurring: £250/month, or £3,000/year if paid annually
- Contract: month to month, no long lock-in; annual option is optional, not required
- Includes: everything in Silver, plus full ongoing SEO, active social media management, monthly performance reporting, priority support and faster updates
- Best for: a business that wants the website actively marketed, not just hosted

## One-off website build (no ongoing plan)

- Price: £500–£1,000, depending on scope and page count
- For businesses that will handle their own hosting
- No recurring fee

## Paid Ads (add-on to Silver or Gold)

- Setup/management: free to add
- Cost structure: ad spend billed directly from the client's own ad account, plus a small fee per lead generated
- No lead, no fee

## AI & automation services (scoped and quoted individually, not flat-rate)

- AI Strategy & Readiness
- Process Automation
- Knowledge Systems
- AI Compliance Readiness (governance/documentation for EU AI Act, ISO 42001, UK GDPR — not legal advice)
- Systems & Infrastructure

Each AI/automation project is scoped to the individual business; there is no published flat rate for these. Get a quote via the contact form at https://adaptumai.co.uk/index.html#contact or take the free assessment at https://adaptumai.co.uk/ai-readiness.html.
